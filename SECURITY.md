# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@vorluno.dev**. Please do not open a public issue.

**We acknowledge every report within 72 hours.** That is a commitment about the acknowledgement, not
about the fix: how long a fix takes depends on what the problem is, and we will not promise a date
we do not control. What we will do is tell you what we found and keep you updated while we work.

Useful things to include, none of them required:

- What you did, and what happened.
- The version you were running.
- Whether you believe it is being exploited.

## What counts

Anything that lets someone read, change or destroy data they should not reach — including through a
dependency of ours, or through a default we ship.

## What does not

- Findings from an automated scanner with no working reproduction.
- Missing hardening headers on a page that serves no data.
- Vulnerabilities in a provider's own API, which belong to that provider.

## Disclosure

Tell us first, and give us time to fix it. We will credit you in the release notes unless you prefer
otherwise, and we will not ask you to sign anything or stay silent afterwards.
